# What is Computer Science?

<em>Computer Science</em> is the study of computation, systems of computation, information, and automation.
<br />
It combines mathematical foundations, engineering practices, and logical analysis to evaluate how computational systems process data and solve complex problems.
<br />
## Key Subfields
<br />
**<u>Data Structures and Algorithms:</u>**&nbsp;&nbsp;Data structures as a subfield is the study of organzation and 
storage of data for efficient access and modification. Algorithms as a subfield is the study of step-by-step logical
procedures used for solving problems.
<br />
**<u>Computational Complexity Theory:</u>**&nbsp;&nbsp;Computational Complexity Theory is the classification computational 
problems according to their inherent difficulty and resource requirements.
<br />
**<u>Software Engineering:</u>**&nbsp;&nbsp;Software Engineering is the study of the design, development, and 
maintenance of large-scale software systems.
<br />
**<u>Databases:</u>**&nbsp;&nbsp;Databases as a subfield is the study of the design, development, and 
implementation of systems to store, manage, and retrieve large volumes of data efficiently and securely.
<br />
**<u>Cybersecurity:</u>**&nbsp;&nbsp;Cybersecurity is the study of protecting computers, networks, programs, 
and data from unauthorized access, damage, or cyberattacks.
<br />
**<u>Artificial Intelligence (AI):</u>**&nbsp;&nbsp;Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the study of 
developing algorithms or models capable of performing tasks that typically require human cognition, 
such as learning, reasoning, and problem-solving.
